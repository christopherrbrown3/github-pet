# Near-touch badge spacing: current #28 revision

Chris's latest direction supersedes the previous ≥3-point compact target: the **visible count capsule should nearly touch the visible mascot**, targeting approximately **1 point of actual contour-to-contour clearance**, without opaque overlap in any current pose or ±5° attention sway. Keep full DEMO and count text, the approved badge fonts/heights, truthful `2+`/`?`/`99+` semantics, dynamic sizing and the saved screen-space ground anchor. This is a design handoff, not a native-rendering signoff.

## Measured starting geometry

The source-contour sampler in [measure-near-touch.py](measure-near-touch.py) evaluates nonzero-alpha pixel edges, actual rounded capsule silhouettes, all current pack frames, and attention rotations from −5° to +5° in 0.1° steps. It uses a **1-point target**, a 0.25-point provisional compositing reserve and a source-space sampling margin; output is quarter-point rounded upward. These are pack-specific starting values, not screenshots or proof of final 1×/2× pixels.

That protects all sampled frames and sway angles, but visibly creates more whitespace in some poses; it alone does not fully meet the near-touch intent. With this fixed full-sway envelope, direct source sampling found `2+` gaps ranging **1.98–3.08 pt at 64**, **1.51–6.12 pt at 128**, and **1.82–12.55 pt at 256** through the sway. Do not call a fixed-envelope 256-point badge a 1-point gap throughout the animation.

## Keeping approximately 1 point across poses and the wave

Use a state/pose-specific visible-alpha contour at the count capsule footprint, rather than a global topmost-row or rectangular canvas bound. For upright states, the measured L values above are starting values. Source sampling at 256 points found the fixed idle envelope gives 1.40–2.67 pt across idle frames, so maintaining the near-touch target through every pose also needs a pose-specific count offset. Retain a fixed panel large enough for its full state or clip envelope, then move the **count capsule within its reserved slot** by a bounded vertical offset derived from the current rendered pose/angle; keep it upright and readable. For idle blinking, update with the pose using the same safe clearance rule; for a static state, set it once. DEMO can remain in its fixed safe position. Precompute a per-frame/per-angle alpha/capsule clearance profile with conservative interpolation; do not scan pixels or load images per animation tick. The nearest capsule-to-art separation should target approximately 1 point, and never permit opaque overlap. At a sprite-frame transition, use a safe envelope for both outgoing and incoming contours while moving the capsule so interpolation cannot cross opaque art. Actual native compositing may need a small extra backing-scale reserve; preserve no-overlap first and report the measured gap honestly.

This count tracking is a deliberate revision of the earlier “labels stationary” gesture proposal, necessary to keep the badge near moving art without varying the artwork's ground anchor. It does **not** authorize moving the hover window on every frame: preserve PM's compact small-size hover (at most two lines, count/quality intact), beside placement and one hover-anchor adjustment at clip start/end when the sway lands. Check the hover against the union of the moving count, fixed DEMO and all art poses, with the #27 0–12-point maximum gap and no overlap.

Under system/app Reduce Motion, the art is static, count motion is disabled and the static-attention contour applies. For quiet hours/snooze, retain existing suppression and do not replay old events. At a genuine state change or clip start/end, update the placement atomically with the pose so an old position never overlaps the incoming art. Unknown/loading/disabled/partial/fresh semantics and privacy remain unchanged. If a pack does not supply trustworthy alpha bounds, a font/localization/accessibility override changes label dimensions, or a larger sway/new frame appears, remeasure that exact pack/pose/label and retain a safe envelope; do not reuse these v1 coordinates blindly or truncate text.

## Evidence and acceptance boundary

The JSON [near-touch-measurements.json](near-touch-measurements.json) preserves measured capsule/contour requirements, candidate state offsets, the safe fixed envelope and sampled gaps for `2+`/`99+`. The sampler uses every current alpha frame, pixel-square boundaries sampled at quarter-source-pixel intervals, and 0.1° angular samples with margins for unsampled angles/edges. It uses the original [AppKit font measurements](native-measurements.json), not invented widths. At rest, sampled nearest count gaps are roughly 1.3–2.7 pt depending on state and quantization. Individual poses within the fixed sway envelope can be farther away, hence the tracking requirement above. A sampled source-contour calculation cannot certify the actual native composited gap or guarantee that text/shadows/backing-scale interpolation never encroach.

Developer and QA must verify actual visible capsule-to-art separation at 64/128/256 and intermediate sizes on 1×/2× backing, for `2`, `2+`, `?`, `99+`, full DEMO, privacy, reduced motion, every idle/urgent frame and continuous ±5° attention sway. Check all-frame non-overlap, no truncation, world-space ground-anchor stability, atomic state changes, and hover placement at screen edges. Pixel comparison should include glyph/capsule antialiasing and any shadows. If native rasterization consumes the 0.25-point reserve, enlarge the reserve as little as needed to keep a positive gap and report the observed actual range.

The earlier [3-point compact proposal](compact-badge-metrics.json), baseline comparison images and prior lane heights are historical; they do not depict this revision. Original generated art and v1 manifest remain untouched. No runtime/board/merge changes are made by Designer.
